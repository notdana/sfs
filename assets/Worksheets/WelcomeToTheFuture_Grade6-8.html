Before you start: open your notebook

This guide doesn't hold your answers. Your engineering notebook does. Grab it, turn to the first blank page, and write today's date at the top. Everything you write here becomes Entry 01.

Work through the four steps below in order. For each prompt, copy enough of the question that it'll still make sense when you read it back next year, then write your answer underneath. There are no wrong answers. This is a snapshot of you, today.

p. 03 Step 03

What Gets Me Going

What to do Here's the big question: why should YOU care about robotics, programming, AI, electronics, design, and entrepreneurship? These skills don't just belong to scientists. They belong to you, whatever you love.
Tip Love sport? Athletes use data and design to train smarter. Love art? Designers and game creators use programming and AI every day. Love helping people? Entrepreneurs and AI builders solve real problems. These subjects are tools for YOUR future, whatever it looks like.

  1. 🌟 What's something you love doing outside of school? A hobby, sport, creative thing, or passion.

    Write it down in your notebook

  2. 🔗 Pick ONE subject from Step 2. How could it connect to something you already love? Even if it sounds crazy, give it a try.

    Write a few sentences in your notebook

  3. 💪 When was the last time you worked really hard at something because YOU wanted to, not because you had to? What was it?

    Write a few sentences in your notebook

  4. 🚀 Imagine it's the end of this year and you've learned something that genuinely blows your mind. What would you MOST love that to be?

    Write a few sentences in your notebook

  5. 🎯 Finish this sentence:

    Copy the sentence into your notebook and complete it
p. 04 Step 04

A Letter to Future-You

What to do This is the coolest part. You'll write a letter to yourself, and a real website will deliver it a year from now. Draft it in your notebook first so a copy stays with Entry 01, then send it.
  1. Go to www.emailtofuture.com on your device.
  2. Set the delivery date to ONE YEAR from today.
  3. Use the prompts below to write your letter. Be honest, be funny, be YOU.
  4. Hit send, then forget about it. It'll arrive like magic. 📬
Tip Write it like you're talking to your best friend. That friend just happens to be you, one year older.

  1. 👋 Who are you today? What are you into right now, and what's going on in your life?

    Draft in your notebook, then type it in

  2. 😄 What are you most excited about for this year? Is anything making you nervous?

    Draft in your notebook, then type it in

  3. 🤝 What's one promise you want to make to future-you?

    Draft in your notebook, then type it in

  4. ❓ What's one question you'd love future-you to answer when the letter arrives?

    Draft in your notebook, then type it in
