1

Lab Preparation

2 months

The physical foundation is built while the school's teacher is brought on board.

  • Procurement of hardware, kits, tools, and devices
  • Lab build and configuration (or build to specification, depending on the setup model)
  • Teacher hiring, the school's teacher is identified and brought on during this window
2

Onboarding

1 month

Everything needed to actually run the program is set up, and the teacher is trained.

  • Operations and documentation systems set up
  • LMS integration and account setup
  • School launch preparation
Includes Teacher Training, Part 1 (2 weeks) plus the first assessment, delivered within the onboarding month, before the first lesson.
★ Ends with the Launch
Launch Lab opens · inauguration · first lessons begin
3

Teacher Training, Part 2

2 weeks · mid-year

The second training deepens the teacher's capability once the program is underway.

  • Second training block, delivered mid-year
  • Second assessment
Certification is earned only after both assessments are passed. The teacher is certified once Part 1 and Part 2 assessments are both complete.
4

Running & Supported

Through the year

The program runs, with structured support and a quarterly rhythm of audits and meetings.

Quarterly audit & meeting

Four structured audits and meetings a year with the person in charge, to review delivery quality and keep the program on track.

Review & Grow

End of year

The year's outcomes are reviewed with the school, the proof of what students learned, and the path forward is set.

  • Full review of student outcomes and program delivery
  • The bridge to continuing and growing the partnership
School point person
One named contact at the school, assigned at kickoff, who anchors the relationship.
Assigned SFS trainer
One trainer from our side who delivers the training and supports the school through the year.
